# Paper accounting and market data

Only **PAPER / SIMULATED** USD stock exposure exists. Every experiment starts exactly $1,000. Cash-funded long positions allow fractional quantity. Default risk limits: 40% combined ticker allocation, 95% total deployment, no negative cash. Same-ticker selections from both researchers are combined before sizing. Costs are included inside the allocated budget.

Decimal accounting uses eight decimal places for internal prices/quantities/cash, round-down quantity sizing and half-even accounting rounding. Displayed money uses two decimal places. Buys apply +2bps spread +3bps slippage; sells apply -2bps spread -3bps slippage. Fixed fee is configurable (default $0). Fill metadata retains raw observation, provider, order/fill time and fill-model version. Immutable cash entries use stable financial-effect IDs; replays do not debit twice. Reconciliation checks cash against the ledger and positions against fills before review.

Unsupported/missing/stale/future quotes cannot produce a fill. Failed exits keep positions OPEN, set RECOVERY_REQUIRED, and block next allocation. Never replace an unsupported delisting/halting exit with a zero value or a fabricated quote. The operator retries during a supported session and extended holding is labeled.

Synthetic prices/history are deterministic hash-based fixtures. Their fundamentals/news are explicitly unavailable. They are safe redistribution test assets. Optional authenticated adapter: Alpaca read-only market data plus security metadata, historical raw daily bars, latest executable trades and SPY comparison. Default IEX is partial-market coverage, not the consolidated market. API keys are required. Assets are screened to an operator-reviewed US common-stock allowlist; ETF/fund/preferred/warrant/depositary/units/trust names are conservatively rejected. Confirm issuer/security classification independently before production use.

[Alpaca market-data documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api) describes feed/subscription coverage. Verify your plan, data delay and redistribution license before publishing real evidence. Unavailable fundamentals, earnings, events/news and automatic corporate-action feed coverage are explicit. Do not interpret an empty field as no event.

Entry/exit comparisons cover submitted eligible proposals over frozen matched raw-price observations; these are hypothetical returns, not additional account trades. The SPY ETF is comparison-only and never an eligible portfolio holding. Account equity is cash plus supported marked holdings; P&L derives from accounting, never model prose. Monthly profit uses realized exit timestamps, and incomplete/open-position equity displays explicit valuation freshness.

Explicit verified split, dividend and symbol-change adjustments have immutable action IDs and quantity/cash reconciliation. Supported partial buys leave unspent cash; partial exits commit supported fills and retain remaining positions for recovery. Automatic corporate-action ingestion, a provider-supplied liquidity model and exchange-specific delisting resolution still require integration before forward unattended use. See the implemented action/partial-fill controls and their limits in verification.md. Historical replay with a modern model would contain possible hindsight and must not be presented as forward evidence.

## Account-free public adapter

`MARKET_PROVIDER=yahoo` uses the publicly accessible Yahoo chart endpoint directly with httpx. No Alpaca account, API key or brokerage funding is required. This is an unofficial endpoint without an uptime or stable-contract guarantee. Keep use personal and the dashboard private; see [public API research documentation](https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/).

The adapter checks USD, US exchange and EQUITY metadata against the configured common-stock universe. SPY is fetched separately as comparison-only. Research uses the last 20 complete prior-session closes and average volume over five prior sessions; it excludes the current partial daily bar. Historical closes may be split-restated and are research inputs only. Fills use the timestamped `regularMarketPrice` observation with configured costs; they do not claim executable broker quotes. Provider publication time is unknown and remains null. Retrieval and observation times and measured age are recorded.

The default maximum observed price age remains 120 seconds. Execution polls for up to 90 seconds for an observation at or after the simulated order. Missing, future, stale and unsupported-session observations cannot fill. Individual failures remain explicit; there is no fallback to synthetic values or yesterday's close. Friday recovery preserves unsupported positions. Reported midweek dividends/splits block exits for affected holdings until independently verified and recorded through the existing explicit corporate-action service. The required immutable action ID is `<experiment-id>:yahoo:<ticker>:<chart-event-kind>:<epoch-date>`. Other supported holdings can still exit. Coverage is not comprehensive; undetected events and issuer classification remain research limitations.

The Yahoo adapter itself omits fundamentals, news, earnings, halts and full corporate-action coverage. Researchers independently browse for research evidence and acknowledge what they cannot verify. Missing execution evidence still blocks fills. Public data is adequate to begin this bounded paper experiment; better data can be added without changing its ledger.
